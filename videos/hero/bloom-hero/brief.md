## Idea
A young founder types prompts into the real AdFlex image form on her laptop. The GPT knot flies out of "Generate", lands on each object around her coffee cart and transforms it: the awning, cups, chalkboard menu (English and Arabic), bus-stop poster and finally a social post on a passer-by's phone. The tagline is "ADS FROM ONE PROMPT".
