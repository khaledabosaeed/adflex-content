# Mammoth Wash: production notes

## Look and sound
- First version: handheld phone footage, 16mm grain, warm golden hour, photoreal.
- Adnan then asked for a **more realistic, documentary** look like the reference: flat overcast light, muted colours, a real camp with background people. A free colour grade was applied. The new hook was generated in that style.
- Sound: a custom trap/tribal instrumental around 95 BPM, cuts on the beat and light SFX.
