# Social references

Viral AI-generated reels, usually with no dialogue: absurd comedy that ends on an AdFlex card.

## What makes a good social reference
- High organic reach: lots of likes and shares, not paid.
- The joke works with the sound off and survives being recast in a Gulf or Levantine setting.
- A strong recurring character or premise we can turn into a series (like الحجة).
- Escalating gags with fast cuts, where the product can live inside the joke.
