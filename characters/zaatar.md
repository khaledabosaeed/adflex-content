## Lessons
- Add diacritics and romanization for hard words (كْلود, يْوَلِّد, المسودة).
- "AdFlex" is pronounced well mid-sentence but gets an extra vowel at the start of a phrase. To fix it, splice a good take of the word in with ffmpeg rather than paying for retakes.
- 6 s of video fits about 8 words.
